docs / overview / about

About

Mohita is a Principal-level technical documentation professional with 11 years of experience writing and architecting documentation across enterprise software, developer platforms, and AI-driven products. Her work spans user guides, API documentation, release notes, installation and deployment guides, online help, and knowledge bases — most recently as Principal User Assistance Developer at Oracle, where she shipped documentation for GoldenGate Studio 26ai and reduced support tickets by 30%.

What sets her apart is the combination: deep structured-authoring expertise (DITA, Oxygen XML, content reuse, topic-based writing) paired with hands-on AI engineering — she designed and deployed a locally-hosted, multi-agent documentation auditor that scores AI-readiness and flags content gaps without sending data anywhere. She teaches this skill set too, running a Udemy course on building API documentation with local LLMs and training other technical writers on AI tooling.

Beyond enterprise documentation, she has developed and published website content for B2B SaaS and product companies, showcasing her range in digital communication and audience engagement. On the research side, she authored a paper that was ranked in the top 10 list of four Elsevier journals — the work is published in a Springer book series and a SCOPUS-indexed journal.

In short: she bridges technology and communication — someone engineers can hand a half-built API to, and someone leadership can trust to own documentation strategy end to end.
